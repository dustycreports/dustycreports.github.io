<p>This course introduces students to the basic requirements of newswriting and reporting, including interviewing, covering news events, speeches and press conferences, public records and more. Students will complete a variety of in-class and outside reporting assignments in journalistic style. (4 credits)</p><p><i>Prerequisite: ENGLWRIT 112. Open to Sophomore, Junior and Senior Journalism majors only. Satisfies the Junior Year Writing requirement and is required for all majors.</i></p>

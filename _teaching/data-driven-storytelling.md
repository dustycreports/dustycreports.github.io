<p>This course teaches students how to: ethically locate and evaluate data, including through the use of public records requests; apply numeracy skills to communicate complex topics to a general audience; conduct data analysis in R; design interactive data visualizations; and report data-driven articles that inform their communities about under-covered topics. (3 credits)
</p><p><i>Prerequisite: JOURNAL 300. Open to sophomore, junior and senior journalism majors only. Counts as a multimedia course for journalism majors in the general track or either the PR or sports concentrations.</i></p>

<p>This class focuses on how journalism serves as a democratic safeguard by exposing corruption, scrutinizing powerful institutions, and fact-checking public claims to prevent the misuse of power. It is centered around three institutions: the first is the electoral system, investigating who is bankrolling politicians and scrutinizing their records as they get ready to run for office in November; the second is the criminal legal system, taking a visit to the local courts to learn about police, prosecutors, judges, and incarceration; the third is city government: how bodies like city councils, planning boards, and mayors' offices make decisions that affect their constituents’ lives. (4 credits)</p><p><i>Prerequisite: JNX 136/ WRT 136 Journalism: Principles and Practice
